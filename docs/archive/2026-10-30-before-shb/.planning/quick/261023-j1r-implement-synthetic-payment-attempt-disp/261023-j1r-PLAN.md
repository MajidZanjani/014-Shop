1. Persist an exact synthetic request with unique merchant/reference and key; replay must match.
2. Archive canonical request bytes and recover them before a durable one-way dispatch claim. Commit uncertainty before calling the fake bank outside a transaction. Never dispatch an already claimed attempt.
3. Reconcile by reference lookup only, with exact correlation and retained prepared bytes. Missing/mismatched observations cannot authorize retry or effects.
4. Verify archive failure, lost responses, concurrent dispatch, strict identity and closed live gates on owned PostgreSQL. Reconcile planning and sync.

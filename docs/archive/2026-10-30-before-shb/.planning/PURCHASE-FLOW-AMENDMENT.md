## Current boundaries

Required purchase inputs are licence scope plus basic platform, applicable existing business/billing/entity/tax/end-use/acceptance requirements. A short listed-kit choice incorporates standard revision/configuration; further variant details are asked only if they change the offer/support/ready-design applicability. Unknown board is valid for an applicable standard development offer; missing licence scope is not.

Seller-controlled scope/price/minimum/support and honest immediate/later delivery are outputs. Support messages are optional or required; missing policy uses existing quote/review. No universal one-package rule or guarantee that ten hours completes a design. Do not silently convert ready design into development. Excluded targets/other-supplier-IP-only requests remain unavailable.

Engineering details and document versions belong to later private support/fulfilment, not universal checkout. Existing contact/address/licensed-entity/invoice helpers remain; optional marketing stays separate. Support covers SLL IP and integration board/FPGA/memory guidance, not unrelated IP/general consultancy. Support credits do not extend licence rights.

Seller inputs remain narrowly pending in P-02/03/05/06/08: initial ready-kit variants, standard R&D scope/price/support minimum, annual start trigger, included/work-credit scope, later delivery commitment and eligible existing-credit ownership/scope. No per-board launch approval is required. Local explicitly synthetic fixtures do not establish actual compatibility or commercial approval.

No new infrastructure/service authority. No required engineering portal, full hardware database, general workflow engine or automated credit pool. Existing protected-service, privacy, archive-before-effects, recovery and financial gates remain. Free Trial IP Form remains excluded.

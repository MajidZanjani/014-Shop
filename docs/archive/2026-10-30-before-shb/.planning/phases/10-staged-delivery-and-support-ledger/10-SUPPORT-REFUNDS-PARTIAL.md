# Reviewed support-refund requests — local partial evidence

2026-10-26, plan10-05, FUL-06/T10; October21 handoff §148 requires review of consumed-support refunds without erasing performed work or silently exposing a negative usable balance. Actual support/refund policy, money/document authority and financial/provider qualification remain pending.

## Implemented scope

A verified active nonstaff owner can request review of a positive integer number of support seconds against one exact archived accepted/captured-payment support grant. The request archives/read-backs its original grant identity/hash, immutable request identity and committed ledger revision/hash with available, reserved and consumed seconds separately. Requests cannot exceed the original granted seconds; this is a resource/scope bound, not a policy declaring any amount refundable. All requests require review. No free text, host/key/technical upload, bank detail, cash amount or customer-supplied eligibility/approval field is accepted. Exact retries preserve the original snapshot even after later support work.

Separate explicit per-case refund assignment plus recent local staff MFA gates append-only `needs_information`, `declined` or `ready_for_financial_review` decisions. Ordinary support assignment alone does not authorize refund-case review. Review archives/read-backs a current committed credit snapshot, actor and prior review hash before publication. Compare-and-swap checks both case review revision and current ledger revision. Review corrections append; older decisions and original work remain. Later ledger activity marks the latest review out of date. Exact review retries retain the historical review rather than silently replacing it with new balances.

Financial-review-ready is not refund approval. Amount, currency, eligible seconds, refund policy, seller approval, bank operation and credit document remain null. There is no refund dispatch, bank call, refund credit hold, grant reduction, work reversal, balance change or original rights change. A request exceeding available credit explicitly distinguishes reserved/consumed credit; it does not allocate either to a refund or declare them eligible. Concurrent support work remains governed by the original ledger and can invalidate review currentness. No pooled credit or automatic negative-balance correction is introduced.

SQL triggers prohibit case/review update/delete; uniqueness protects per-grant request identities and case review revisions/retry identities. Case limit1000/grant, review limit1000/case, integer bounds and20-row private history/list pagination keep local operations bounded. Owner summaries omit staff identity/generation/MFA/archive metadata. Each read verifies source grant/ledger and relevant retained archives.

Customer routes: `/purchase/checkout-rehearsal/<checkout>/support/refunds/` and case detail below that path. Staff routes: `/staff/support-refunds/` and exact assigned case detail. Existing support/staff home links and shared base/shop stylesheet provide no-JavaScript private forms. Closed JSON/form schema, normal CSRF/origin enforcement and private cache/referrer policy apply. Local operator `grant-refund`/`revoke-refund` use `--case`; reset/revoke remove refund grants and rotate/revoke the existing credential. No production staff bootstrap or new financial execution route.

# Supporting Hours Bundle

Engineering support for the use and integration of supported SLL IP. Select whole
10-hour FPGA bundles or request reviewed 25-hour ASIC bundles. A bundle purchases
engineering time, does not buy an IP licence and does not guarantee a result.
No response-time or resolution-time SLA applies unless separately agreed.

Purchased FPGA hours do not expire. ASIC no-expiry terms remain proposed until
adopted in the applicable ASIC supplement. Invitation expiry never cancels a
purchased service. The support team keeps time records and manually emails the
authorized customer contact after each support task; the shop shows only the
hours in the current purchase and has no online support balance or bundle panel.

FPGA eligibility is established by SLL before an automatic-purchase invitation is
issued. ASIC requests receive technical review before an exact offer can be
accepted and purchased. Submitting a request does not charge the customer or
reserve engineering availability. No technical files, credentials or design
uploads are required at checkout or initial ASIC request.

Support concerns supported SLL IP. New IP development, major new functionality,
unrelated RTL/third-party IP, full system/chip development, PCB design/layout,
signal integrity, certification, on-site services, travel, hardware and separate
development projects require a separate agreement. ASIC offers do not imply
tape-out, sign-off or foundry/EDA/PDK access. Customer material remains confidential
and customer-owned under the applicable agreements. Support transfers no SLL IP
ownership or additional licence rights; the customer remains responsible for its
design, validation, compliance and suitability.

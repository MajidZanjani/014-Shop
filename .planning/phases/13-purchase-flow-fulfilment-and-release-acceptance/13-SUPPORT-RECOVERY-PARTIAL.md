## Behavior

A normal CSRF-checked request could therefore reserve credit, release a reservation or add a fixed private follow-up after quarantine, including a completed quarantine/release in that gap. The resulting response also exposed the ledger/follow-up under stale access.

The existing view now supplies its originating customer request explicitly to ledger/follow-up commands and reads. Their own durable transactions lock the current valid session epoch before owner/resource work and hold it through local archive verification and commit. This also gates exact command retries and both private read transactions. Fresh verified sessions can resume the exact original request and retry without another event/context revision.

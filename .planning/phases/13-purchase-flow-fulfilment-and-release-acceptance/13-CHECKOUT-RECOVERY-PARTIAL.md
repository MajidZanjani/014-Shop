## Behavior

The checkout view checked customer access before parsing, then passed only its cached owner to acceptance, payment, lookup, grant and staged-delivery services. A quarantine or completed quarantine/release in that gap could still change acceptance, payment-attempt/provider observations, licence/support grants or delivery completion. A completed release could allow a new fake bank start under the fresh global epoch even though the customer request belonged to the old session.

The view now supplies its originating customer request to each existing transaction boundary. The shared checkout authority predicate locks the current valid epoch before owner/resource work; the support ledger delegates to that same predicate while keeping its exact existing error/privacy contract. Checkout review and accepted-record reads use the predicate too. Internal trusted callers retain their no-request interface and current fake-only restrictions.

Acceptance checks both durable transactions around archive work. Grant and staged-delivery preparation remains archived first, then the existing publication transaction rechecks the epoch before completion. A reset during archive work leaves prepared evidence or an unaccepted confirmation; it cannot turn that preparation into accepted money, rights, support credit or delivery completion. Fresh verified customers can resume the exact retained identities without duplicate effects.

Payment captures the valid customer session epoch and passes it to the existing dispatch admission. Admission compares that optional UUID against the current unheld epoch while holding its existing lock. A stale bound prepared attempt denies before new archive/claim/provider work and never adopts a released epoch. A quarantine after the one-way claim preserves uncertainty; nested provider admission denies while held, unfinished admissions block release, and a later lookup miss cannot authorize a resend. Internal read-only reconciliation remains usable while held. No bank/lookup I/O runs under caller database locks.

Existing deadlines, immutable snapshots, archive checks, one-way claims, exact retries, staged obligations, staff MFA/resource scope and actual-offer holds remain. No model, migration, dependency, runtime pin, timeout, public route or actual provider operation is added. The new optional admission argument defaults to existing internal behavior.

This is a local customer domain/dispatch boundary. It does not qualify actual traffic/process isolation, confidential intake, post-point revocation reconstruction, MFA recovery/custody, real merchant operations or independent off-host restore. No full Phase13 SUMMARY or actual approval is justified.

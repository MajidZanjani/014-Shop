# Protected offer/domain contract — proposed, not implemented

This resolves the next design boundary in [07-02](07-02-PLAN.md) and extends [07-EVIDENCE-CONTRACT](07-EVIDENCE-CONTRACT.md). It does not approve an offer or authorize runtime implementation before its named prerequisites exist. Source: COM-02–06, the October21 T07 contract, the immutable embedded [product](../../../docs/product-source/SLL_xSPI_MC_Product_Page.md) and the single [P register](../../../docs/decisions.md). Current implementation inspected at b96b665; only request/preparation and local prepared archive facts exist.

## Actual interfaces and authority

QuotationRequest retains CartSnapshot and a versioned QuotationBusinessScope. QuotationReview and PreparationAssessment retain internal decisions/assessments with actor/MFA and exact scope references. None is an approved offer. offer_preparation.assess always returns blocked, null amounts/deadlines, pending inputs and disabled action flags; its payment_due_at field is advisory, not an implemented payment-start deadline. document_preview.preview provides source declaration labels and applicable pending documents, not approved text or accepted versions. StaffQuotationGrant is a reviewer assignment with one exact positive business scope; it grants no commercial approval role.

RequestEnvelope freezes request-only bytes; RequestArchiveIntent and selected local claims/readback retain prepared evidence with false retention/effect flags. Successful capture, readback, reconciliation or275 local checks cannot promote these records into protected offer, acceptance or completed domain evidence. A future domain envelope requires its own operation/revision/schema identity and adapter qualification. Reuse reviewed primitives where suitable; do not create a parallel catalog, approval authority or generic conversion of request receipts.

## Proposed exact candidate schema

The following is a closed field contract for a future immutable candidate; no model/table, schema identifier, approval record or validator exists yet. Candidate formation is internal and never a customer payable offer. Choose and test a bounded canonical encoding before implementation; do not assume the request-only131072-byte limit accommodates approved text and encrypted-host references.

| Field group | Required retained values and constraints |
|---|---|
| identity | Schema version, candidate UUID/revision, quotation UUID, owner/resource scope, retained creation time; distinct domain envelope UUID/kind/revision. Semantic uniqueness is the candidate aggregate/revision, independent of browser retry keys. |
| request_scope | Exact cart snapshot identity/version, source/product/profile/catalog/private revision references, ordered line UUIDs and complete retained line configurations. The reviewed selection is explicit and recoverable. A later selection/edit produces a new candidate; no implicit line dropping/splitting/merging/repricing. |

Retain per-line component identities, rate-card revision, unit/quantity, integer minor-unit amounts, annual/one-time/renewal basis, tax category/rule and rounded result under approved calculation policy. Eventual paid/accepted package credit is granted once through its own ledger boundary and never renews IP rights. |

Missing future bank expiry alone does not prevent internal candidate preparation. |

Approval is a separate immutable decision binding candidate identity/revision, exact byte digest, every required commercial/policy/document revision, approver principal/resource role, current MFA credential generation/time and decision time. Preserve recoverable bytes as well as hashes. Revocation/correction is retained as a new event, never an overwrite. A retry may recover the original decision only after current exact-resource authorization; no reattribution or authority refresh. Approval role assignment, recovery and freshness rules require their own actual contract; reviewer grants and the synthetic MFA rehearsal cannot stand in for it. Separation-of-duty requirements, if any, remain pending policy rather than an invented second approver.

## Transition and concurrency contract

1. **Internal blocked candidate:** missing approved inputs remain explicit. A complete internal review does not remove commercial blockers. No publication, acceptance, collection or licence/support effect follows.
2. **Frozen candidate awaiting protected approval:** reconstruct the exact selected source/cart/entity/host/component/tax/document/deadline revisions under compatible short aggregate locks. Persist recoverable immutable bytes and pending reasons with semantic uniqueness/CAS. No external I/O under locks. Runtime lock order must be designed against actual approval/host services; current request lock order alone does not qualify that integration.
3. **Protected decision and pending domain archive:** a permitted approver explicitly binds the exact frozen candidate after current scope/MFA/role and prerequisite rechecks. Persist decision and its distinct durable domain archive intent atomically. An unknown/commercially incomplete candidate cannot acquire an approve decision. Internal request-byte capture cannot satisfy this transition.
4. **Archive-qualified, unpublished:** outside database locks, retain the actual decision/offer content in the authorized destination. Record actual destination/object version/hash/size, recoverable content and required retention evidence. Recheck domain identity, lease/generation, cancellation/revocation and exact approved revisions in a short completion transaction. Local prepared evidence stays pending/held for domain qualification even if its technical readback succeeds.
5. **Explicit guarded publication:** recheck current resource approval authority, candidate/decision revisions, source applicability, validity and independently qualified domain evidence. Retain publication identity/time as a distinct durable fact before exposure. No automatic transition from archive readback to customer visibility. Revocation while I/O is in flight prevents publication; already externally observed effects require reconciliation, not deletion.
6. **Later acceptance/order:**07-03 must accept only the explicitly displayed approved revision/selected line set and all applicable exact declarations/documents, with signatory/authority/version/hash/time evidence. One approved offer revision yields one acceptance/order across sessions and changed retry keys. Mixed scopes retain both applicable IP/support sets; support-only has owner/positive quantity and applicable general/support/refund terms without IP declarations. Marketing remains separately optional and initially unticked. Acceptance is not bank dispatch, paid status, delivered licence or spendable support credit. Archive each actual domain fact at its required boundary; prepared future evidence cannot prove completion.

Database restoration must block exposure/effects until external money/acceptance/documents/licences/support are reconciled and restored access/MFA invalidated. This contract does not implement those recovery controls. No new production storage/worker/runner or protected-host operation is authorized by this design.
